# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 27, 2026

## Overview

Kai is a private AI assistant that runs primarily on your device. This policy explains how your data is handled when you use the app.

## Data Collection & Storage

**Kai does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.** The app has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no advertising SDKs.

All of your data — conversations, settings, memories, emails, SMS messages, notifications — is stored **locally on your device** in app-private storage.

## AI Providers

Kai connects to AI providers **only when you explicitly configure them** (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or your own compatible endpoint).

When you send a message to an AI provider:
- Your message is sent to that provider's servers
- The provider's privacy policy governs how they handle that data
- You can choose which provider to use at any time

## Permissions

Kai requests certain Android permissions for optional features. These are never used without your explicit action:

- **Microphone** — voice input and wake word detection
- **Notifications** — reading and sending notifications for AI automation
- **SMS** — reading and sending SMS messages (you must explicitly enable SMS tools)
- **Calendar** — reading calendar events (you must explicitly enable calendar tools)
- **Contacts** — reading contacts (you must explicitly enable contact tools)
- **Location** — GPS location (you must explicitly enable location tools)
- **Phone** — device information and call state

All permissions can be granted or revoked at any time in Android system settings.

## Email

If you configure email (IMAP/SMTP), your credentials and emails are stored locally on your device. Kai connects to your email provider only when you explicitly trigger email-related actions.

## Sandbox

The built-in Linux sandbox runs locally on your device. Files you upload or the AI creates within the sandbox remain on your device.

## Data Deletion

All app data can be deleted by:
1. Clearing app data in Android system settings
2. Uninstalling the app
